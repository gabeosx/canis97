## External Research

- **Trademark/name screening:** USPTO guidance requires evaluating similarity in appearance, sound, meaning, and commercial impression across related goods/services, plus broader common-law searches; an exact-match search cannot establish clearance. Sources: [likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion), [comprehensive clearance searches](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks), and [federal trademark searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching).
- **Provider marks and disclosure:** SiriusXM identifies its names and logos as protected marks and grants no implied mark license. Independent naming, limited factual references, and a non-affiliation disclosure reduce confusion risk but do not supply permission or cure a confusing identity. Sources: [SiriusXM Web Terms](https://www.siriusxm.com/sxm-web-terms-of-use) and [Customer Agreement](https://www.siriusxm.com/customer-agreement).
- **Current macOS app icon:** Xcode 26 supports an Icon Composer `.icon` file as the primary layered app-icon source; Apple recommends a simple 1024×1024 layered composition with system-owned masking and appearance rendering. Sources: [Icon Composer setup](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/creating-your-app-icon-using-icon-composer) and [App icon HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons).
