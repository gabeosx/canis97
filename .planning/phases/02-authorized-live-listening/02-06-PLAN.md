## Phase Goal

**As a** SiriusXM subscriber, **I want to** find my entitled linear channels and reliably control one live stream, **so that** I can listen on my Mac with truthful playback and metadata state.

<objective>
Make the one live session recover conservatively from expiry, network interruption, sleep/wake, and stalls without racing newer commands or amplifying protected responses.

Purpose: Preserve dependable listening while making every automatic provider action bounded, classified, and cancelable.
Output: Client re-resolution cancellation guarantees, injected coordinator recovery policy, native event observers, and deterministic recovery/failure tests.
</objective>
