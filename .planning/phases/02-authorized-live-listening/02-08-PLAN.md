## Phase Goal

**As a** SiriusXM subscriber, **I want to** find my entitled linear channels and reliably control one live stream, **so that** I can listen on my Mac with truthful playback and metadata state.

<objective>
Preserve the subscriber's active session through ordinary compatibility failures and make every fixed live resolution and redirect classification operation-scoped.

Purpose: Close the session-loss and actor-reentrancy blockers without broadening the provider contract or creating another authentication path.
Output: Non-mutating operation revalidation, explicit-only erasure authority, per-resolution tune/resource/key context, task-local redirect tracking, and deterministic concurrent regressions.
</objective>

| Operation-time entitlement result → active session actor | Volatile provider classifications may reject one operation but must not acquire implicit logout or erasure authority. |
