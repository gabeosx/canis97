## Decisions Made

- Favorite-song rows use a separate `FavoriteSongRow` and are never selectable channel rows, preventing Tune, queue, entitlement, Now Playing, and star semantics from leaking into the collection.
- `FavoriteCurrentSongActionState` owns all Player-menu semantic copy so disabled reasons and saved/removal presentation stay closed and app-owned.
- The pasteboard writer is injected at the library boundary and writes only the saved `snapshot.copyText` projection.
