## Decisions Made

- Song identity is deterministic Unicode/case/whitespace-normalized title plus artist only; album and source channel are display context, not identity.
- Album remains nil at the action boundary because the current client contract has no verified semantic album field.
- Durable store failure returns a closed failure and never republishes an optimistic song favorite.
