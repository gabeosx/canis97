<objective>
Deliver the production song-favorite tracer and its durable domain: one coordinator-confirmed current song can travel from discrete semantic metadata through the existing session controller into a separate SwiftData record and become truthfully saved only after durable success.

Purpose: Prove the authority and persistence boundaries before adding the collection UI, while preserving the existing channel favorite, playback, system-media, and skin contracts.
Output: Pure favorite-song models, separate SwiftData storage and projection, confirmed-current eligibility, a distinct Player-menu action, closed announcements, offline contract coverage, and compile-only Xcode evidence.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
primary_noun: normalized (title, artist) song identity for song favorites; LiveChannelID remains the primary noun for channel favorites
decision: no-change to existing channel identity and FavoriteRecord; add a separate song-favorite record/projection alongside the channel collection
rationale: The domains have different identity and playback semantics. A generalized Favorite identity would erase the non-tunable song/channel boundary and contradict D-04, D-09, and D-11. Contract coverage must prove channel favorites remain channel-shaped and song favorites cannot tune or join playback queues.
</assumption_delta_decision>
