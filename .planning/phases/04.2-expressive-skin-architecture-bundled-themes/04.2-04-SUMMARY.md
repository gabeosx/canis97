## Deviations from Plan

### Human-review corrections

The original checkpoint plan expected review-only work. Human inspection identified that flat color-and-line treatments did not establish logical control wells, several native glyphs and labels were visually off-center, text could escape the painted displays, and independent marquee clocks could hitch when several rows moved together. These were correctness and acceptance fixes within the phase scope, not expanded skin authority.

**Impact:** The corrections strengthened the public declarative skin proof without adding executable skin behavior, external dependencies, networking, or new product scope.

## Issues Encountered

- Initial faceplate rounding cropped generated artwork; the faceplates were regenerated and fitted to the complete compact silhouette.
- Pocket Disc and Aqua Vista semantic controls did not initially coincide with their illustrated wells; shared native alignment geometry and exact slot frames corrected the mismatch.
- Long channel and song values exceeded their display areas; bounded marquee rows now clip and scroll while retaining complete accessibility values.
- Multiple independently animated marquee rows could appear jerky; all rows now share one animation timeline and move already-laid-out composited text.
