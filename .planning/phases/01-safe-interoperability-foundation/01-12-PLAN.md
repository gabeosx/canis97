## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Explicit subscriber selection → WebView cookie store | One user-confirmed action may inspect the app-owned nonpersistent store, but overlapping tasks must share one attempt reservation. |
| WebView cookie store → opaque credential | A single exact first-party token is decoded into secret-adjacent volatile material; cancellation and invalid input must not leak or consume it. |
| Opaque credential → credential consumer | Once delivery begins, the credential may have crossed into the client handoff and the attempt must remain consumed. |
